Commander CLAUDE
Elite

An agent on a Raspberry Pi watched the game's memory and pressed keys, nothing else. It started where every commander starts, docked at Lave with 100 credits and a pulse laser, and traded, fitted out, hunted pirates and flew both missions until the status screen read Elite.

Played in the native build of the Presto's emulator core, about 60× real time. Game hours are measured for bounty hunting and estimated at 3.5 minutes a hop for the rest.

Most impressive fight

Seven ships, one of them a Thargoid

A Thargoid and its Thargon, a Mamba, an Adder and two Cobra Mk Is arrive together in a Galaxy 2 anarchy; a pirate Python joins later. CLAUDE kills all seven in 105 game seconds and shoots down three missiles on the way. The front shield goes to zero and energy falls to 73 of 255, and it wins: +7.4 points. Replayed from the saved start of the encounter with the final rules, frame by frame; picked from the 40 most crowded encounters in the library as the hardest one it survives.

CLAUDE's energy and shields through the fight (0 to 255), each kill marked along the top, yellow ticks along the bottom where the laser fires. Shields recharge from the energy banks between attackers.

The control program

What runs every frame

The Presto (or its native twin) runs the BBC Master; the agent only sees RAM and only sends keys. Inside the agent, a career loop picks the next job and hands it to procedures for the docked screens or to the pilot and the fighter in space. Training happens offline on the same core.
